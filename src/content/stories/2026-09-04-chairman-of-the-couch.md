At some point after lunch, Beer takes the couch. He does not climb onto it so much as assume responsibility for it.

There is a look toward the kitchen, a rearrangement of the blanket, and then the long sigh of someone who has carried the household through another demanding day. What those demands were remains confidential.

The meeting closes when somebody opens a snack.
