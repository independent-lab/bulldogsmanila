The first lap is enthusiasm. The second is commitment. By the third, Bowser has forgotten why the running began and decided that this is no reason to stop.

A toy moves. A cushion shifts. Beer watches with the expression of an older brother who has seen civilization decline in real time.

Then Bowser falls asleep where the race ends.
