There are places in a home that remain claimed. A patch of afternoon light. The quiet end of the room. The spot from which the whole family could be watched without making a fuss.

Bacon’s place is not empty so much as remembered. The family grew around his calm, and some part of that steadiness is still here—inside the routines, inside the way we notice one another, inside home itself.

Love leaves evidence.
